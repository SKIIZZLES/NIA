Child safety standards

Android app app.nia.mobile — last updated: 30 September 2026

NIA has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Designated child safety contact: niaapp@outlook.com

1. What is prohibited

  • Any image, video, illustration, text or link that shows, describes or suggests a minor in a sexual context, including drawn, computer-generated or AI-generated content.
  • Any sexualisation of a minor: sexual poses, comments, captions or hashtags.
  • Soliciting a minor for sexual purposes (grooming), including attempts to contact them outside NIA.
  • Asking for, trading or selling intimate images, and sextortion (blackmail based on intimate images).
  • Trafficking or exploitation of children in any form, and any content promoting it.
  • Sharing such content, even to denounce it.

More broadly, NIA bans all nudity and all sexual content for every user (see the community guidelines).

2. Prevention

  • Minimum age of 13 to create an account, stated at sign-up in the Terms of Service.
  • A general ban on nudity and sexual content, including for content labelled 18+.
  • Anyone can block an account from its profile.

3. How to report

  • In the app: tap ⋯ → Report on a video or photo, the flag next to a comment, or the flag at the top of a profile.
  • By email, with or without an account: niaapp@outlook.com, giving the username and a description of the content. Never attach the content itself.
  • To the authorities, in France: PHAROS (internet-signalement.gouv.fr) for illegal online content; 119 for a child in danger; 17 or 112 for immediate danger. Elsewhere, contact the police or child protection services in your country.

Do not download, screenshot or share child sexual abuse material, even to report it: possessing and distributing it are offences.

4. What we do

  • Reports involving a minor are reviewed with top priority.
  • Any confirmed child sexual abuse material is removed and the account that posted it is deleted, with no right to create a new one.
  • The facts are reported to the competent authorities, including PHAROS in France, and we respond to requests from authorities within the legal deadlines, including removal requests within 24 hours.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement within the framework of legal procedures.

5. Compliance

NIA complies with applicable child safety laws, including French law (such as the Law for Confidence in the Digital Economy) and the EU Digital Services Act (DSA), as well as the Google Play Child Safety Standards.
