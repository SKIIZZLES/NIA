Delete my account

Android app app.nia.mobile — last updated: 29 September 2026

Option 1 — from the app (immediate)

  1. Open NIA and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to "Delete my account", below "Sign out".
  4. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Option 2 — by request

If you no longer have access to the app or to your account, write to niaapp@outlook.com with:

  • subject: NIA account deletion;
  • the email address linked to the account;
  • your username, if you remember it.

We verify the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account within 30 days at most. Nothing is kept beyond that, apart from the technical backups described below.

What is deleted

CategoryDetail

What is not deleted, and why

Notifications already received by other people stay in their history, but they lose every link to your account: your name no longer appears in them. Erasing them would mean altering other users' data.

Our host's technical backups may hold a residual copy for a short period, until their rotation cycle overwrites it. Those backups are not reachable from the app.

Deletion cannot be undone. Your videos, your username and your followers cannot be recovered afterwards. Creating a new account with the same email address does not restore them.
