6. Reporting, blocking and moderation

Reporting. Every video, photo, comment and profile can be reported from the app: ⋯ → Report on a post, the flag next to a comment or at the top of a profile. Anyone, even without an account, can also report content by writing to niaapp@outlook.com, with the username involved and a description. The person reported is not told who reported them.

Blocking. Every account can be blocked from its profile. Blocking someone removes their content from your feed. It does not stop them from seeing yours: a published video stays public.

Moderation. Reports are reviewed by a person; those involving a minor or a serious threat get top priority. Depending on severity, we may remove content, restrict or suspend an account, or delete it. A serious breach — in particular any content involving a minor — leads to immediate deletion of the account and, where appropriate, a report to the competent authorities.

Child sexual abuse material. It is removed and reported to the authorities, including — in France — PHAROS (internet-signalement.gouv.fr), the official platform for reporting illegal online content. If you see any, do not download, screenshot or share it: report it in the app and to PHAROS or your local authorities. In immediate danger, call 17 or 112; for a child in danger in France, 119.

Appeals. If you believe a decision was wrong, write to niaapp@outlook.com explaining your situation. We will look at the case again and reply.

NIA hosts its users' content; it is not the author of that content and does not approve it one by one before publication. What you see in the feed comes from other people, and it is their responsibility, not ours.
