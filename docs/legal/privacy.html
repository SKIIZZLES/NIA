1. What NIA is

NIA is a vertical video app for African and Afro-diasporic creators. It lets you publish videos, watch them, and interact with other people's.

2. Data we collect

Only what you provide or produce by using the app. Nothing is collected in the background.

3. What we do not collect

  • No location data. The app never requests the location permission and includes no geolocation library. A video's "region" field is text you choose, not a GPS reading.
  • No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics. No such SDK is bundled in the app.
  • No advertising identifier is read.
  • No health, financial or biometric data, and no contacts from your address book.
  • We never sell or rent your data, to anyone, under any circumstances.

4. Device permissions

Requested when you need them, never at launch.

Denying a permission does not stop you using the rest of the app: you can watch, comment and follow without ever granting camera access.

5. Where your data lives, and who can reach it

Your data is hosted with Supabase (PostgreSQL database and file storage), our only technical subprocessor for hosting. We pass your data to no other third party.

If you choose to sign in through an external provider (Google, Snapchat), that provider sends us the minimal identification details you authorise. These sign-in methods are optional; email and password always remains available.

Public in the app: your username, display name, bio, profile picture, published videos and comments. Private: your email address, archived videos, saves, blocks and reports.

6. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Database access is controlled row by row with PostgreSQL Row Level Security policies: an account can only read and change what it is entitled to.
  • Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth. We have no access to them.
  • The API key embedded in the app is a public key with no administrative rights.

No system is infallible. If a data breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the competent authority within the deadlines set by applicable law.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from the app: Profile → Delete my account. Deletion is permanent.

It erases: your account, profile, videos and files, likes, comments, follows, reposts, saves, series, events, reports and blocks, along with the notifications your actions triggered for other people.

The account, profile and content disappear immediately. Video and image files are erased right after, within about ten minutes, with a second sweep an hour later; copies cached for delivery disappear once the cache expires.

One exception: reports filed about you by other people are kept for moderation. They hold neither your name nor your content.

If you no longer have access to the app, use the account deletion request page.

Our host's technical backups may keep a residual copy for a short period before their rotation cycle overwrites it.

8. Your rights

  • Access and correction — from Profile → Edit profile.
  • Deletion — from Profile → Delete my account, or through the request page.
  • Objection and portability — write to the contact address above.

If you live in the European Union, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

9. Children

NIA is not intended for people under 13, nor below your country's minimum legal age if it is higher. We do not knowingly collect data about them. If you find such an account, report it to the contact address and we will delete it.

10. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new update date. Substantial changes will be announced inside the app.
